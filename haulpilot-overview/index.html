01 — Overview

What is HaulPilot?

Most small operators run their business across WhatsApp, dispatcher calls, screenshots, spreadsheets, and memory. HaulPilot replaces that mess with one tool that answers the money question on every load: should I accept it, negotiate it, or reject it?

Who it is for

Launch wedge: non-CDL box truck owner-operators in the US, from solo drivers to fleets of 2–15 trucks.

Product scope: any small carrier running brokered loads and needing load-level profit visibility.

What it is not

Not a GPS tracker. Not ELD software. Not a dispatcher. Not QuickBooks. It is the financial decision layer that sits between the load and the operator's wallet.

Main gap

Before accepting a load, operators have no fast way to know if it is actually profitable after current fuel, deadhead, driver pay, tolls, dispatch fee, and broker/payment risk.

Why start with box trucks if the same product helps other small carriers?

The core workflow — Profit Light, Broker Shield, Load Wallet, Fleet Scoreboard, and Weekly Money Meeting — works for any small carrier that accepts brokered loads and needs to know if each load turns into real profit. Box trucks are the launch wedge, not the ceiling.

02 — How it works

The load lifecycle.

Every load follows the same money path. HaulPilot sits at every step.

01

Load offer arrives

The operator forwards a WhatsApp message, rate confirmation PDF, broker email, screenshot, or voice note to the Load Inbox.

02

AI extracts the load

Pickup, destination, rate, miles, broker, MC number, times, and terms become structured data.

03

Profit Light gives a decision

Accept, negotiate, or reject based on real CPM, fuel, deadhead, tolls, driver pay, and weekly goals.

04

Broker Shield checks risk

Authority, identity, suspicious domains, payment history, ACH changes, and network warnings are checked before pickup.

05

Run the load

Status, notes, receipts, documents, and driver updates attach to the load timeline.

06

POD, invoice, payment

Proof of delivery triggers the invoice, payment tracking, reminders, and actual-profit closeout.

07

Weekly Money Meeting

Every Monday, the system tells the operator what made money, what lost money, who owes money, and what rule to follow this week.

03 — Product modules

Six connected modules.

Load Inbox

Forward messages, PDFs, screenshots, or voice notes. HaulPilot turns messy load offers into clean records.

Profit Light

The core decision engine. It shows accept / negotiate / reject, minimum rate, and target counteroffer.

Broker Shield

Broker identity, authority, domain, payment, dispute, and network-risk checks before the operator commits.

Load Wallet

Every load has its own money timeline: expected profit, invoice, due date, paid status, and set-aside suggestions.

Fleet Scoreboard

For 2–15 trucks: per-truck P&L, driver performance, maintenance cost per mile, unpaid invoices, and utilization.

Weekly Money Meeting

The AI coach: one clear weekly summary and one rule to protect margin.

04 — Go-to-market

Use the dispatcher network as the pilot engine.

First 30 days

Interview 10 operators, onboard 5 live beta users, collect 100 real rate confirmations/load examples, and manually validate the Profit Light + Broker Shield workflow.

Why your friend's contacts matter

Warm operator trust lowers customer acquisition cost. His dispatcher workflow gives real load data, broker communication patterns, and a credible introduction into the first user group.

First market

Russian/Eastern European box truck operators in Chicago/Midwest. Specific, reachable, underserved, and close to your network.

Expansion path

Year 2: cargo vans + hot shot. Year 3: CDL semi owner-operators and small specialty carriers with extra compliance and integration features.

Bottom line.

HaulPilot should not be positioned as generic trucking software. It should launch as the financial brain for box truck operators, then expand horizontally to every small carrier that lives load-to-load and needs to know what actually became profit.
