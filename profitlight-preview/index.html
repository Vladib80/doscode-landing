One core loop: load offer to paid profit

The product becomes useful because it follows the operator's real daily workflow, from the moment a load offer hits the phone until the money lands.

1

Load offer arrives

Operator forwards a rate con, broker email, WhatsApp message, screenshot, or voice note.

2

AI extracts terms

Pickup, dropoff, rate, miles, times, commodity, broker, and payment terms become structured data.

3

Profit Light decision

Live CPM, deadhead, fuel, driver pay, tolls, and goals produce accept / negotiate / reject.

4

Broker Shield check

MC/DOT, authority, email domain, payment history, and network warnings create a risk signal.

5

Run the load

Status, GPS timestamp, notes, receipts, and driver updates attach to the load timeline.

6

POD and invoice

One-tap POD capture generates the invoice and starts payment tracking automatically.

7

Load Wallet

Cash is split into operating, tax, maintenance, truck replacement, and owner draw suggestions.

8

Weekly Money Meeting

The system tells the owner what changed, what to avoid, and what to do next week.

The product modules

Not another generic trucking system. This is financial control around every load.

📥

Load Inbox

Forward rate confirmations, broker emails, screenshots, and voice notes. The app turns messy load offers into clean decisions.

🚦

Profit Light

Accept, negotiate, or reject based on actual cost, deadhead, fuel, driver pay, broker risk, and weekly goals.

🛡️

Broker Shield

Risk indicators for broker identity, payment behavior, suspicious domains, ACH changes, and network-reported issues.

💵

Load Wallet

Every load has a money timeline: expected profit, invoice status, payment due date, and set-aside suggestions.

📊

Fleet Scoreboard

For 2-15 trucks: per-truck P&L, driver scorecards, unpaid invoices, utilization, and cashflow.

🧠

Weekly Money Meeting

AI turns the week into clear actions: minimum rate, bad brokers, weak trucks, late invoices, and next moves.

Built for each growth stage

Solo owner-operator

The owner is in the truck. The product must be phone-first: load scan, Profit Light, Broker Shield, receipts, POD, invoice, and payment reminders.

Small fleet owner

The owner may still drive Truck #1 but now needs per-truck P&L, driver settlements, unpaid invoice board, and broker history across all trucks.

Growing fleet

The owner manages from a desk. The web app becomes primary: cashflow, utilization, driver scorecards, sub-users, and weekly financial coaching.
