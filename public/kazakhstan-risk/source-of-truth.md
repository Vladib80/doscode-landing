## What we check

- Kazakhstan legal entity details: name, BIN, registration date, address, status, activity profile.
- Ownership, director, affiliate, and address signals where available.
- U.S. and international sanctions/watchlists: OFAC, U.S. CSL, BIS Entity List, Denied Persons, Unverified List, plus EU/UK where feasible.
- Russia-affiliation signals: owners, directors, phones, emails, domains, websites, addresses, Russian-language web footprint.
- Company credibility: site, employees, office/warehouse signs, operating history, public footprint.
- Product and shipment risk: BIS Common High Priority List relevance, dual-use sensitivity, route/payment/end-user clarity.
- Red flags: new post-2022 entity, mismatched business activity, vague end user, unusual payment, vague freight route, order volume inconsistent with profile.

## Report output

A concise PDF memo with:

1. Entity snapshot.
2. Watchlist/sanctions screening results.
3. Russia-affiliation and diversion red flags.
4. Product/shipment risk notes.
5. Evidence links and source dates.
6. Risk rating: Low / Medium / High / Critical.
7. Recommended next step: proceed, request more documents, escalate to counsel, or pause.
8. Limitations and disclaimer.

Important rating lesson from KTE.KZ sample:
Separate ratings into:
- Entity / counterparty risk.
- Product / shipment risk.
- Data completeness / confidence.
